Questions people ask

Can I get a mammogram, a FIT kit or a pap test without a family doctor?

Breast screening is the exception: in Newfoundland and Labrador you can book a screening mammogram yourself if you're in the eligible age range. A FIT kit, a pap test, bloodwork and most other checks still need a requisition from a doctor or nurse practitioner. That's the part Kedge does: a licensed clinician reviews what you're due for and sends the requisitions, so you can book the tests.

What does it cost?

Registering is free and doesn't commit you to anything. We're still working out the best way to bring this to people in Newfoundland and Labrador, and we'll set out plainly how it works before you book a visit, so there are no surprises.

Will I be taken off Patient Connect?

No. Kedge doesn't replace a family doctor and doesn't affect your place on the list. When you're attached, we hand your screening record to your new provider.
