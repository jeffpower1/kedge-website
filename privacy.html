Privacy Policy

Effective 24 July 2026 · Kedge Health, St. John's, Newfoundland & Labrador, Canada

Kedge Health helps people in Newfoundland and Labrador keep on top of routine preventive screening. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, where it is stored, and the rights you have over it. We have written it in plain language on purpose.

Two separate things. The waitlist on this website collects only a name and email so we can tell you when we launch. The Kedge Health app collects health-screening information to generate your recommendations. They are kept entirely separate, and the sections below cover each in turn.

1. Who we are

Kedge Health is a preventive-screening service founded and operated by Dr. Jeff Power, a physician in St. John's, Newfoundland and Labrador. For any privacy question, or to exercise any of the rights described below, contact privacy@kedgehealth.com.

2. The website waitlist

When you join the waitlist on this site, we collect the name and email address you enter, along with the date you signed up. We use this only to email you about Kedge's launch and availability.

  • This is marketing data, not health data. It is stored separately from any clinical system and never contains health information.
  • It is held in our own Google Workspace and is not sold, rented, or shared with advertisers or data brokers.
  • You can be removed at any time — reply to any email from us, or write to privacy@kedgehealth.com, and we will delete your entry.

3. The Kedge Health app

The app asks you a set of structured questions — such as your age, sex at birth, and health details relevant to routine screening (for example, smoking history or family history) — and applies published Canadian screening guidelines to identify which routine tests you may be due for.

During the current beta

Kedge is being tested through Apple TestFlight before public launch. Testers should enter test information, not real patient records. No real clinical care is delivered through the beta.

What the app does with your information

  • It generates a list of screening recommendations for you. A clinician reviews every recommendation before anything is acted on — the software orders no tests and diagnoses nothing on its own.
  • It provides safety guidance: if you report urgent symptoms, the app stops and directs you to appropriate care (such as 911 or 811) rather than to screening.
  • Notifications we send you contain no test names or results — only a prompt to log in securely.

How your information is protected

  • Stored in Canada. Health information is hosted on Canadian servers (AWS Canada, ca-central-1) and handled under Newfoundland and Labrador's Personal Health Information Act (PHIA).
  • Encrypted. Information is encrypted in transit and at rest, with sensitive fields individually encrypted using AES-256-GCM.
  • No advertising or tracking. The app contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it to target ads.
  • Guest mode keeps information in memory on your device only and does not persist it to an account.

4. When we share information

We do not sell your information or share it for advertising. Health information from the app is shared only:

  • with the clinician(s) involved in reviewing your recommendations or your care;
  • with service providers who host or secure the system on our behalf, under contract and bound to protect it (for example, our Canadian cloud host); and
  • where required or permitted by law, such as a legal obligation under PHIA.

5. Your rights

Under Newfoundland and Labrador's Personal Health Information Act, you have the right to access the personal health information we hold about you, to ask us to correct it if it is wrong, and to raise a concern about how it has been handled. To do any of these, contact privacy@kedgehealth.com.

You may also contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for Newfoundland and Labrador (oipc.nl.ca) if you believe your privacy rights have not been respected.

6. Retention

Waitlist entries are kept until we launch and you have been contacted, or until you ask us to delete yours, whichever comes first. Health information collected through the app is retained only as long as needed for your care and to meet our legal and professional record-keeping obligations under PHIA, and is securely deleted or de-identified after that.

7. Children

Kedge Health provides adult preventive screening and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under the age of majority in their province of residence.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date at the top and, for material changes, notify people through the app or by email. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Questions about your privacy? Email privacy@kedgehealth.com. Kedge provides information and navigation support, not medical diagnosis. If you have a medical emergency, call 911.
